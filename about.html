About

Researcher of memory,
literature & heritage

Saumya Aanchal is a researcher, writer, and editor based in Patna. Her work moves between English literary studies, cultural history, and democratic discourse, held together by a long attention to the heritage of Bihar and eastern India — its painting, its rivers and rituals, its half-forgotten scripts.

As Assistant Editor at The Review of Democracy, published by the Central European University's Democracy Institute, she convenes scholarly book panels, produces academic podcasts, and writes on memory politics and populism in contemporary India.

She returns, again and again, to how communities remember — through ritual, through water, through script.

That impulse shapes her books. She has authored a study of Patna Kalam painting and edited a multidisciplinary volume on Chhath Puja, both for the Indian National Trust for Art and Cultural Heritage, and her research extends to documenting the Kaithi script, the historical writing system of Bihar and eastern Uttar Pradesh.

She holds an M.A. in English Literature from Patna University and has qualified in UGC NET (2026) and GATE (2025). She is also a trained classical vocalist, holding a Junior Diploma in Indian Classical Music.
