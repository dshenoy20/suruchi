About

Researcher of memory,
literature & heritage

Saumya Aanchal is a researcher, writer, and editor based in Patna, India. Her work spans English literary studies, cultural history, and democratic discourse, with a consistent thread of archival and field research into the heritage of Bihar and eastern India.

She is Assistant Editor at The Review of Democracy, published by the Central European University's Democracy Institute, where she organizes scholarly book panels, produces academic podcasts, and writes on memory politics and populism in contemporary India.

Alongside this editorial work, she has authored a book on Patna Kalam painting and edited a multidisciplinary volume on Chhath Puja, both for the Indian National Trust for Art and Cultural Heritage (INTACH). Her research also extends to the documentation of the Kaithi script, the historical writing system of Bihar and eastern Uttar Pradesh.

She holds an M.A. in English Literature from Patna University and qualified in UGC NET (2026) and GATE (2025) in English. She is also a trained classical vocalist, holding a Junior Diploma in Indian Classical Music.
